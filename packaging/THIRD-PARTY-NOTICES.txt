FFmpeg (ffmpeg.exe, ffprobe.exe)
--------------------------------
This package bundles FFmpeg binaries built by the BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds project
from FFmpeg master, in the LGPL configuration (no GPL-only components).
Muse runs them as separate command-line processes to read video metadata and
to extract thumbnail frames.
